### 1. Add Paywall Design

1. **Look for "Paywall" or "Design" section**
2. **Click "Design Paywall" or "Create Paywall"**
3. **Design your paywall:**
   - Choose template or start custom
   - Add your branding
   - Products should sync from RevenueCat (through your code)
   - Customize colors, fonts, layout
4. **Save**

### 2. Configure Limits (Optional but Recommended)

**In the "Limit" section:**

1. **Click "+ Add Limit"**
2. **Set frequency capping:**
   - **Max per day:** 3 (prevents showing too often)
   - **Max per week:** 10
   - **Cooldown:** 24 hours between shows
3. **Save**

**This prevents annoying users with too many paywalls!**

### 3. Configure Entitlements (Recommended)

**In "Entitlements" section:**

1. **Change from "All users" to "Users without entitlements"**
2. **This means:** Only show paywall to users who DON'T have premium
3. **Select:** "Users without entitlements" or "Non-subscribers"

**Why?** Don't show paywall to users who already have premium!
