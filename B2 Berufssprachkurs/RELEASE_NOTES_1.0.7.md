# Release Notes - Version 1.0.7

## English

### What's New in This Version

• **New Subscription System**: Integrated RevenueCat and Superwall for improved subscription management and paywall experience

• **Holiday Seasonal Banner**: Added special holiday promotional banner to the paywall

• **New Pricing**: Updated subscription prices for monthly, yearly, and lifetime plans

• **Enhanced Animations**: Added shimmer effect to word of the day and pulse animations to learning cards

• **Share Feature**: New share functionality to share the app with friends

• **UI Improvements**: Enhanced dark mode support and overall user interface refinements

• **About Section Updates**: Improved About section with app name and version information

• **Bug Fixes**: Fixed subscription button product IDs and various stability improvements
