# Content Rights Guide for App Store Submission

## Question: Does your app contain, show, or access third-party content?

### Understanding Third-Party Content

**Third-party content includes:**
- Content from textbooks, educational materials, or publishers
- Images, videos, or audio from other sources
- Text, translations, or explanations copied from books or websites
- Content licensed from educational institutions or publishers
- User-generated content from other users
- Content accessed via APIs from third-party services

**NOT considered third-party content:**
- Content you created yourself (original vocabulary lists, original translations)
- Content that is factual/public domain (basic vocabulary words, common translations)
- Content you have explicit written permission/license to use
- Content that is your own intellectual property

### Scenario B: Content from Educational Materials/Textbooks
**Answer: Yes, it contains third-party content**

**If:**
- Vocabulary lists are from a specific textbook
- Translations or explanations are copied from published materials
- Example sentences are from copyrighted educational materials
- You don't have explicit written permission to use the content

**Then select:**
```
Yes, it contains, shows, or accesses third-party content, 
and I have the necessary rights
```

**AND you must:**
- Have written permission/license from the copyright holder
- Be able to provide proof of rights if Apple requests it
- Have proper attribution if required by the license

## Important Notes:

**If you select "Yes" (third-party content):**
- Apple may ask for proof of rights
- You must have written permission/licenses
- You may need to provide attribution in the app
- Some content may require different handling in different countries

**If you select "No" (no third-party content):**
- Make sure you're confident in your answer
- Content should be your original work or public domain
- Common vocabulary and basic translations are typically OK
- Following a curriculum is OK if you create your own content
